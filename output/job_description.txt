Position Information


Job Summary


Looking for a Lead Full Stack Software Engineer to join our NextGen PAS team. This team
is spearheading the effort to develop strategies for migrating multiple legacy applications
from a mainframe environment and building new cloud-native applications in an open-
source environment.


We are seeking an engineer to take on a central role as the lead for application design and
development. The ideal candidate must have extensive experience with open systems, from
requirements definition through to development, design, testing, and release. The required
skill set is correspondingly high, and the successful candidate must demonstrate excellence
in several of the following areas:


Extensive experience in designing and developing open systems, including databases.


· Code analysis of existing systems
· Knowledge and experience with public clouds (Azure, AWS, GCP)
· Experience in data and UML modeling
· Ability to review code created by team members and provide appropriate feedback
· Experience in the life insurance industry and related business knowledge
· Communication with other teams primarily in Japanese
· Ability to lead teams of up to "two pizzas" in size.


Responsibilities


To migrate the functions of existing applications currently running on a mainframe to
an open system, the incumbent will be responsible for carrying out all necessary tasks
from the architecture planning, design, development, testing, and deployment of new
Java Batch/UI/API applications.


We hope that in the future, ideal candidate will be able to take the lead in planning and
developing teams, providing guidance to members, and conducting reviews of
deliverables.
