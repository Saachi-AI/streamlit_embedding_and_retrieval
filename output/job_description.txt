AI and Data Governance Manager


We are seeking an experience and strategic AI and Data Governance Manager to lead the
development and implementation of sustainable governance practices within our fast
paced, innovative and global environment. The ideal candidate would bring at least 5
years of proven experience in managing AI and Data governance in a large organization.
If you have demonstrated experience in creating and adapting AI and Data related
frameworks in collaboration with key stakeholders worldwide, and turning them into
successful operations, we would be delighted to talk to you.


This role requires native level proficiency in Japanese and business proficiency in English
as well as excellent visual communication skills to effectively communicate with a variety
of audiences from executives to technical teams. The ideal candidate will be a proactive
fact finder, effective and efficient project manager, able to analyze simple data in Excel,
highly proficient in visual presentation tools such as Power Point and creation of artifacts
in multiple formats. Familiarity with data, analytics, artificial intelligence and IT
environments is valued.

Communication and Presentation: Articulate complex governance concepts

Agile Problem Solving: Address and resolve governance challenges swiftly and

technologies.
Awareness and Influence: Participate in a variety of relevant forums to develop
