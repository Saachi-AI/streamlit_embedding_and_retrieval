Job description


We are looking for a talented System Operations Engineer to join our team. This
role is a mix of DevOps and Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) responsibilities,
aimed at building and supporting business applications while ensuring uptime
and system performance.


Responsibilities:


- Design, build, and maintain the infrastructure and configuration management
needed for continuous integration, continuous deployment, and continuous
delivery (CI/CD) pipelines.


- Collaborate with development and operations teams to ensure smooth


deployment and operation of systems.


- Monitor and optimize system performance, reliability, and scalability.
- Implement best practices for security, monitoring, and alerting in all aspects of
infrastructure and application development.


- Automate repetitive tasks to improve efficiency and reduce human error.
- Troubleshoot and resolve issues in development, testing, and production
environments.


- Participate in on-call rotation to provide support for production systems.
