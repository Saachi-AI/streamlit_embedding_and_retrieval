Job description


Job Purpose


We are looking for a Lead/Senior Scrum Master who will act as a True Leader,
driving Agile transformation and fostering a culture of continuous improvement.
You will empower teams to deliver value with flexibility, enhance cross-team
communication, and help shape a more Agile organization.


Key Responsibilities


1, Empower Teams to Reach Their Full Potential
- Foster a strong team spirit and encourage collaboration.
- Guide the team toward self-organization and maximizing its potential.
- Coach the team on Agile principles and best practices to enhance value
delivery.
- Create a safe environment for open discussions and issue resolution.
- Promote a Kaizen (continuous improvement) mindset in daily work.


2,Support Growth & Learning
- Identify and facilitate growth opportunities such as knowledge sharing,
feedback -sessions, and mentoring.
- Coach the Product Owner in backlog management, prioritization, and
stakeholder communication.
